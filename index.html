How AMD Mart Works

Buy safely. Pick up directly. No delivery charges.

Tap to see processTap to hide
  1. 1

    Request a Product

    Found something you like? Tap “Request to Buy” and send your request.

  2. 2

    Seller & Admin Review

    The seller confirms it’s available. The admin verifies it exists at the seller’s stated location.

  3. 3

    Both Confirm

    Seller and admin approve. Then AMD Mart shares the seller’s full contact and pickup details with you.

  4. 4

    Collect Your Product

    Visit the seller’s location, check the product and collect it directly from them.

Important:
  • AMD Mart does not provide home delivery. Buyers must collect products directly from sellers.
  • No advance payment, no hidden charges and no delivery charge, not even ₹1.
